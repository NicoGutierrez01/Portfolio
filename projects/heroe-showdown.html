ABOUT THE PROJECT

FIGHT. COOPERATE. SURVIVE.

Héroe Showdown is a 2D multiplayer action and fighting game built around competitive and cooperative gameplay. Players can face each other in PvP battles or join forces in PvE to defeat waves of enemies and a powerful final boss.
